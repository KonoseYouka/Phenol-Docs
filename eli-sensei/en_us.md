# Description of the bot program for Teach Us, Eli-Sensei!
⚠️Currently, Eli-sensei is in beta. It may be unstable.

## 1. Personal Data
This program (hereinafter, Eli-sensei) collects the following information in the channels `#eli-sensei` and `#eli-deletion`:
- Your Discord user ID and username
- Messages you send to Eli-sensei
- The date and time you interacted with Eli-sensei and its content

The collected data is used to provide Eli-sensei's features and improve its quality. Personally identifiable data will not be disclosed to third parties (other than the service provider and yourself).

## 2. Data Deletion
You can unregister anytime with `/unregister`. After sending the command, you can choose one of two deletion methods.
1. Delete everything … Deletes your account info and all your questions Eli-sensei is holding.
2. Account only … Deletes your account info, but keeps your questions, with the poster info (Discord ID and username) anonymized.

Either way, your data is kept for 7 days after you unregister, and then permanently deleted.

If you use `/register` again within 7 days, everything is restored to how it was. The deletion method choice is reset too.

### ⚠️About questions that have already been adopted
The fan site itself is managed by a separate system, not Eli-sensei.

An adopted question has already been handed off elsewhere. Unregistering cannot retract or delete it. Please keep this in mind before agreeing.

## 3. Others
Eli-sensei may change or discontinue the service without prior notice.
